Supported formats and limits

  • Input formats JPG, PNG, WEBP
  • Output format Same as input
  • Max file size 20 MB
  • Max upscale 4× (AI, device-dependent)
  • Longest side supported Up to 4000 px
  • Max output size ~35 MP (e.g. 7680 × 4320)
  • Processing location Your browser, on-device

When you choose 2× or 4×, QualityUpgr tries to run a real local AI super-resolution model (a small ESRGAN-family network, via TensorFlow.js) directly in your browser — it reconstructs plausible detail rather than just stretching pixels. It first tries your GPU (WebGL); if that isn't available it falls back to a slower CPU pass; if AI can't run at all on your device, it falls back to real browser-native resampling plus unsharp-mask sharpening instead — never a silent trick. Whichever engine actually ran is always labeled honestly under your result. At 1× nothing is resized — you just get sharpening and contrast/detail adjustment on the original dimensions.

Your files stay under your control.

Photos you enhance with QualityUpgr are processed directly in your browser, on your own device. They are not uploaded to a QualityUpgr server for this local processing to happen, and this page makes no network request containing your image.

The first time you use 2× or 4× AI upscaling, your browser downloads a small AI model file (code and numeric weights, not related to your photo) from a public CDN so it can run locally; after that it's cached by your browser. Your photo itself is never part of that or any other request.

Closing or reloading the tab clears everything — nothing is kept after your session.
