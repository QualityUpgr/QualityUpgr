Supported formats and limits

  • Input formats JPG, PNG, WEBP
  • Output format Same as input
  • Max file size 20 MB
  • Max upscale 2×
  • Longest side supported Up to 4000 px
  • Processing location Your browser, on-device

Enhancement uses classic sharpening (unsharp masking) plus contrast and saturation adjustment, then real browser-native resampling for upscaling. It isn't an AI model reconstructing missing detail — it's making the detail that's already in your photo more visible. 4× upscaling isn't offered yet because the result didn't hold up to a real quality bar.

Your files stay under your control.

Photos you enhance with QualityUpgr are processed directly in your browser, on your own device. They are not uploaded to a QualityUpgr server for this local processing to happen, and this page makes no network request containing your image.

Closing or reloading the tab clears everything — nothing is kept after your session.

We're building QualityUpgr.

QualityUpgr is a new, independent project focused on making photo and video enhancement simple, honest, and accessible. Today it does one thing well: real, local photo sharpening and upscaling.

We're starting small and improving it step by step — video enhancement is next.
